- **8 Race-17 Tools (`tools.json`)**: `ItemRead` (full card; unknown id -> `{"trouble_message": "E49"}`), `BuyerAsk` (2 replies, then `{"error": "budget_exceeded"}`), `PassNow` (declines), `ItemsLocate` (search: 12 rows from the row offset `Lead` 0..60, `hit_found` capped at 500, `page_next_page` = `Lead + 12` while more rows remain and `Lead + 12 <= 60`, `Lookup` <= 120 chars), `ListMyItems` (`current_bag` in the order the lines were first held + `owed`), `RemainingNow` (message meter `reto`), `SetToCart` (`place` ADDS `NumberNeeded` (default 1) to the line, a line over 4 -> `E50`; `remove` subtracts `NumberNeeded` (default 1), never below 0; unknown id -> `E49`; answers `{id, line_qty}`), `OrderNow` (an empty basket -> `E49`).
- **Split view**: an `ItemsLocate` row is `can_buy, details_text, id, producer_name, section_path, seller, title, unit_price`; `facts` and `remarks_section` (the seller's blurb, where the pointer sentences and pairing rules sit) appear only on `ItemRead`.
- **Compatibility is strict**: a car part must SAY it fits (`details_text` opens with `Fits: TOT nnnn.`; a listing that names no car is not compatible - 212 of 212 field-accepted car-line listings say it); a bulb fits a floor lamp when its part series (title `(part VI4-6335)` -> `VI4`) is one the lamp takes (`details_text`: `Model number: LIR8320. Takes part series VI3, VI7.`, plus the series the page of the lamp's `More on part VI4-1035.` bulb adds: `Model LIR3350 also takes part series VI4.`).
- **Reference chains**: a reference names a page by seller, shelf and attributes; decoy pages of the same seller sit beside it (`A version with ... exists elsewhere.`). The pointer sentence is in `remarks_section` (`For socket, look to another product, the CPU from Shizenlen with ddr variant ddr3 and processor speed 31ghz, not to this rod.`). A need revealed by reference comes in three frames: `designed to pair with the one the page ... points to or states` (the page states a value AND a pairing rule, e.g. `Our rule for ddr variant: ddr3 goes with ddr5, ddr4 goes with ddr4, and ddr5 goes with ddr3.`; the need is the PARTNER), `something apart from ...` (any other value), `not under ...` (at least that amount).
- **Store Notices**: three triggers - `first_add` (the first `SetToCart` that leaves a line in the basket), `after_reveal` (first need revealed), `finish` (first `OrderNow` / `PassNow`); kinds `budget` (the whole-order cap moves to the amount the sentence names: 6 raises, 4 cuts), `drop` (a stated requirement is waived), `stock` (the first listing held sells out - SILENT on `t-9da2989139`: no notice unless the order is the very next call; the card then shows `can_buy: false`). A notice echoes on the NEXT call as `{"pehidu": [<sentence>...], "tuzaju": <obs>}` (`tuzaju` = the bare reply string under `BuyerAsk`). Everything not yet fired fires at the first close, which is refused once (`{"observation": {"was_fine": true}, "refusal": [...]}`); a close right after a refused close always goes through.
- **Persona**: 12 musician / card-player reveal frames (`simrace/race2/shopper2.py` `_R17_REVEAL_FRAMES`), 10 no-preference + 10 did-not-understand replies; a task answers every off-need question from ONE pool (`dnu_default` on 19 multi-line tasks), no one-at-a-time pool; 2 messages.
- **Process rules** (derived from 9,050 labelled field orders): every hidden need revealed via `BuyerAsk`; every page of a reference chain opened via `ItemRead` (`answer.refs` stated chains - waived in the slots where the store dropped the chain's property; `answer.need_refs` chains of needs revealed by reference, orders only); EVERY ORDERED LISTING opened at some point (`process_rules: ["ordered_listing_opened"]`). 0 of 4,520 orders the field graded anything but `process_requirement_missed` breaks one of the three; every process-missed order with its needs revealed and listings opened lacks a chain page (or sits on one of the 3 never-revealed-need tasks). A decline is never process-missed (0 of 1,863 released declines).
- **Grading**: `changes missed` = an order that would have stood without the store's change (an acceptable listing at the wanted quantity) holds the sold-out listing (field 30 of 30) - an order that was never acceptable stays `request not met` (field 1 of 1); `not the best option` = acceptable but above the lowest total (on lowest-price tasks); race 17 has no `needs not found` label (an order that violates a revealed need is `request not met`).
- **Not reproduced**: a call the runtime rejects before the world (`invalid_arguments`) does not spend one of the task's steps in the simulator's step meter (live: every call counts toward `max_steps`); after the step budget the simulator answers `budget_exceeded` instead of closing the session.
- **No acceptable basket derivable** (0 field passes; the clauses + field carts leave a line empty; a decline is still graded request_not_met as in the field): `t-31e0748262`, `t-a3eddd07e6`.
- **Never-revealed need** (every order is process-missed, as in the field): `t-257c52dc8b`, `t-31e0748262`, `t-e8316716d9`.
